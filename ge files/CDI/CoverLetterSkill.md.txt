# Cover Letter Writer (CV Guide Method)

## Description

Generate a professional English cover letter by following the methodology described in the CV Guide.

This skill does not simply summarize a resume.

Instead it:

1. Analyzes the job description.
2. Identifies the most important responsibilities and qualifications.
3. Matches them to evidence found in the candidate's CV.
4. Researches and incorporates company-specific motivations when available.
5. Builds a persuasive narrative around achievements, impact, initiative, leadership, curiosity, or problem-solving.
6. Produces a tailored cover letter written in professional English.

---

## Required Inputs

### Resume

The candidate CV.

### Job Description

The full job posting.

### Optional Inputs

- Company name
- Hiring manager name
- Industry
- Desired tone

---

## Step 1: Analyze The Job Description

Extract:

### Key Responsibilities

Identify the 3 to 5 most important responsibilities.

### Required Qualifications

Identify:

- Required qualifications
- Preferred qualifications
- Nice-to-have qualifications

Rank them by importance.

---

## Step 2: Match CV Evidence

Create a qualification mapping table.

| Job Requirement | CV Evidence |
|----------------|------------|
| Requirement | Relevant experience |
| Requirement | Achievement |
| Requirement | Project |

Use wording as close as possible to the job description.

Never invent qualifications.

---

## Step 3: Identify Strong Themes

Determine 2 or 3 themes from the candidate's experiences.

Possible themes:

- Leadership
- Initiative
- Curiosity
- Problem Solving
- Teamwork
- Managing Conflict
- Adaptability
- Technical Expertise
- Ownership
- Customer Focus

Select themes supported by evidence from the CV.

---

## Step 4: Company Motivation

Identify:

- Company mission
- Values
- Products
- Recent initiatives
- Industry position

Build genuine motivations linked to the candidate's interests and career goals.

Avoid generic statements.

---

## Step 5: Build The Cover Letter

### Section 1: Introduction

State:

- Who the candidate is
- Relevant experience
- Interest in the position

### Section 2: Transition

Summarize a major achievement.

Format:

"Over the last X years/months, I have..."

Include measurable impact whenever available.

---

### Section 3: Qualification Match

Present the two strongest qualification matches.

For each:

1. Theme
2. Context
3. Actions
4. Results
5. Lessons learned

Prioritize impact and outcomes.

Use specific examples.

---

### Section 4: Why This Company

Explain:

- Why the company is attractive
- Why the mission resonates
- Why the industry interests the candidate

Link motivations to experiences whenever possible.

---

### Section 5: Conclusion

Reaffirm:

- Interest in the role
- Fit for the position
- Enthusiasm to discuss further

End professionally.

---

## Writing Rules

- English only.
- Professional tone.
- ATS-friendly.
- Do not invent information.
- Prefer achievements over responsibilities.
- Prefer quantified impact.
- Avoid generic language.
- Avoid repeating CV bullet points.
- Keep length between 50 and 100 words.

---

## Output Format

Dear Hiring Manager,

[Introduction]

[Achievement-Based Transition]

[Qualification Match #1]

[Qualification Match #2]

[Why Company]

[Conclusion]

Kind regards,

[Candidate Name]

---

## Success Criteria

A successful letter:

- Feels personalized.
- Demonstrates company knowledge.
- Connects qualifications to requirements.
- Highlights measurable achievements.
- Uses evidence from the CV.
- Reads like a persuasive story, not a CV summary.
